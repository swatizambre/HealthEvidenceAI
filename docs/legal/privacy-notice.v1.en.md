# Privacy notice (v1) — HealthEvidence AI

**Not legal advice.** Facility counsel must review before a real-patient pilot.

**Version:** `privacy-notice.v1`  
**Languages:** English (this file), Hindi, Marathi.

## Who we are

HealthEvidence AI helps you keep a lifelong health record and share it with doctors you choose. The facility operating this instance is the **Data Fiduciary** under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).

## What we collect

- Identity and contact details you provide (name, phone, optional ABHA).
- Health information you upload or that clinicians enter: visits, conditions, medicines, allergies, labs, imaging, procedures, vaccines, family history.
- Consent records, access logs, and rights requests.
- Technical logs needed for security (IP truncated, device/session identifiers).

We do **not** use your health data to sell advertising.

## Why we process it (purposes)

1. **Continuity of care** — show your lifelong chart to you and clinicians you authorize.
2. **Emergency card** — show critical allergies, medicines, and contacts when you enable it.
3. **Share with a doctor** — time-limited links you create and can revoke.
4. **Prior-auth / cashless paperwork** (hospital module) — only with a purpose-specific consent.
5. **Security, audit, and legal compliance.**

## Your rights (DPDP)

Access a copy of your data, correct it, withdraw consent, raise a grievance, and nominate someone. Use **My record → Privacy** or contact the grievance officer named by your facility.

## Retention

Clinical records may be retained as required by medical-record law even after you ask for erasure; erasure requests are handled as a workflow with a recorded outcome (see product docs).

## Cross-border viewing

Data for Indian pilots is intended to be stored in India. Viewing abroad by a travelling doctor requires facility counsel sign-off.

## Contact

Ask your facility for the named **Data Protection Officer** and **Grievance Officer**. This notice template does not appoint them.
