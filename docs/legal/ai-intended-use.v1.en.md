# AI intended-use statement (v1) — HealthEvidence AI

**Version:** `ai-intended-use.v1`  
**Not legal advice.** Obtain counsel’s opinion on whether CDSCO would treat this as a regulated medical device before any clinical marketing claim.

## Intended use

HealthEvidence AI **organises and shows health records with sources** so patients and clinicians can find allergies, medicines, visits, and reports faster.

Optional AI features may:

- Phrase a short chart brief from retrieved records.
- Answer desk/chart questions using only provided contexts and citations.
- Suggest where documentation gaps appear for paperwork prep.

## Explicit non-uses

The product **does not**:

- Diagnose disease.
- Prescribe or recommend treatment.
- Approve or deny insurance coverage or cashless authorization.
- Replace a licensed clinician’s judgment.
- Submit claims or NHCX transactions autonomously.

## Human oversight

Senior clinician review remains required for prior-auth HITL acknowledgment. Patients confirm extracted items before they become settled chart facts (when extract confirmation is enabled).

## Labelling

UI and API copy must distinguish **rule check** (deterministic) from **AI check** (model-assisted). Default local mode is rule-based.
