# Genetic data policy (v1) — HealthEvidence AI

**Version:** `genetic-data-policy.v1`  
**Not legal advice.** Align with ICMR guidance and facility counsel before any genetic feature goes live.

## Principles

1. **Separate consent** — Genetic results require a purpose-specific consent distinct from general chart continuity.
2. **Never shared with insurers or employers** through this product’s share or TPA flows.
3. **Genetic counselling** before serious inherited-risk results are shown to the patient.
4. Emergency card may later show limited **gene–drug alerts** (e.g. CPIC) only after clinical pharmacologist validation — not enabled in this demo.

## Current product state

The data model leaves room for genetic results. No pharmacogenomics engine is live. This document is a policy template for Phase 6 work.
